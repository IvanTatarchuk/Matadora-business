# Competitor Research: ConTech / Construction-Management SaaS in Poland & CEE

**Date:** 2026-07-27
**Author:** Automated research (Claude Code)
**Purpose:** Follow-up survey of the competitive landscape for construction-management SaaS in Poland and neighboring EU/CEE markets, benchmarked against Matadora's core modules (VAT-aware offer/estimate builder, investor↔contractor↔wholesaler marketplace, KSeF e-invoicing, multi-role dashboards). This is a follow-up to the [2026-07-06](./2026-07-06.md) and [2026-07-13](./2026-07-13.md) reports — it adds one Polish direct competitor not previously profiled in detail, updates international pricing models, and tracks the KSeF mandate's rollout status.

Research was performed via web search; where a source could not be directly verified (site blocked direct fetch, or only a single secondary source existed), it is flagged inline as unverified/uncertain rather than stated as fact.

## 1. New this cycle: SimplyBud, a third Polish all-in-one competitor

Prior reports named **SimplyBud** only in passing (as one of several bootstrapped Polish incumbents). This cycle surfaced enough detail to profile it alongside budow.app and BudoApka:

**SimplyBud** — marketed as "the first comprehensive construction-management system in Poland," built by Adrian Jochymczyk (Grupa Struktura), targeting construction companies and developers rather than solo contractors. Scope is broader than BudoApka's field-journal focus: client/offer/investment pipeline from first contact through handover, real-time schedule updates, on-site photo/document reporting, and a document-versioning module that auto-notifies all project participants when a file changes. Positioned as CRM+ERP for construction firms. Pricing is **implementation-package-based, starting at 5,000 PLN** (a setup/onboarding fee model, not a transparent monthly-subscription number like BudoApka's), with a free account tier available at app.simplybud.pl for initial signup. No evidence found of KSeF integration, a multi-contractor bidding marketplace, or a wholesaler role.
Source: [SimplyBud](https://simplybud.pl/)

**Why this matters:** SimplyBud sits closer to Matadora's investor-pipeline and offer-tracking use case than budow.app (which is journal/estimating/KSeF-first) or BudoApka (which is field-documentation-first). None of the three Polish players found across all three reports combines VAT-aware estimating, KSeF, and a bidding marketplace in one product — that combination remains unclaimed in the Polish market as far as this research has found.

## 2. Pricing model shifts among international players

- **Procore** has moved further from any published-list pricing toward a pure Annual Construction Volume (ACV) quote model: reported real-world ranges are now ~$4,500/yr (small-contractor entry) up to $60,000-80,000+/yr for mid-market/enterprise, plus commonly-cited $50,000-150,000+ first-year implementation costs and reported 5-14% annual renewal increases. This reinforces the prior reports' read that Procore is priced out of reach for most Polish SMB contractors and developers.
- **Buildertrend** has reportedly removed its three published tiers (previously Essential/Advanced/Complete at ~$339-$1,099/mo) in favor of a 5-step custom-quote flow keyed to annual construction volume, plus a separate $400-1,500 onboarding fee and 12-month lock-in on annual billing. Some older tiered pricing is still cited by third-party trackers, so the transition may be gradual/regional; treat the volume-quote model as the current default and the old tiers as legacy/unconfirmed-current.
- **PlanRadar** pricing is now most consistently cited in EUR (Basic ~€26, Starter ~€89, Pro ~€129 per user/month) rather than the USD figures in the 2026-07-13 report — consistent with its Austria/EU home base, but still no confirmed PLN-specific pricing or Polish-market discount tier.

**Why this matters:** all three international incumbents are trending toward opaque, sales-assisted, volume/seat-scaled pricing rather than transparent self-serve numbers. This widens the gap for Matadora and Polish-native competitors (SimplyBud, BudoApka) to compete on pricing transparency and self-serve signup, particularly for the small-to-mid Polish contractor segment that these enterprise tools are pricing out.

## 3. KSeF mandate: rollout continues, with targeted extensions

Confirming and extending the 2026-07-13 report:

- The Feb 1, 2026 (large taxpayers, >200M PLN turnover) and Apr 1, 2026 (most other VAT-registered businesses) mandatory phases are both now in effect.
- **New this cycle:** the requirement to include the KSeF invoice number in payment transfers has been **postponed six months, to 31 July 2026** — i.e., it takes effect right around this report's date. Worth flagging as an imminent compliance deadline for Matadora's own KSeF module and for any customer-facing messaging.
- Small-scale/low-value invoice issuers retain a workaround (non-KSeF methods permitted) until **30 September 2026**, and an **offline mode** remains available to all taxpayers through the end of 2026 — meaning full enforcement has more soft edges through 2026 than a simple "mandatory since April" headline suggests.
- Micro-entrepreneurs (monthly sales under 10,000 PLN) remain on the **Jan 1, 2027** deadline, per a June 2026 amendment, unchanged from the prior report.

**Why this matters:** the compliance surface is more granular than "on/off" — Matadora's KSeF module should be checked specifically against the July 31, 2026 payment-reference requirement (does Matadora's invoicing/payments flow already stamp the KSeF number where required?), not just against invoice issuance.
Sources: [Dudkowiak & Putyra — E-Invoicing in Poland](https://www.dudkowiak.com/tax-law-in-poland/e-invoicing-in-poland-ksef/), [VATupdate — KSeF mandate briefing](https://www.vatupdate.com/2026/05/02/poland-ksef-e-invoicing-mandate-a-comprehensive-guide/)

## 4. Ecosystem note: construction incumbents are now actively sourcing ConTech startups

**Budimex** (one of Poland's largest general contractors) ran an accelerator cycle this year that selected 15 startups from 100+ applicants, with ConTech as the second-largest category (48 submissions) behind general "advanced construction tech" — spanning quality-monitoring systems and new construction materials as well as software. This signals that large incumbent contractors are actively scouting for ConTech partners/vendors rather than only building in-house, which is a potential enterprise sales channel worth tracking (not a direct competitor signal, but relevant to go-to-market).
Source: [MyCompanyPolska — Budimex accelerator](https://mycompanypolska.pl/artykul/budimex-otworzyl-program-akceleracyjny-prym-wioda-startupy-ai-cleantech-i-contech/19073)

The public-tender/procurement marketplace landscape (Eurobudowa, Atlas Przetargów, Kompas Inwestycji, e-Zamówienia, Platforma Zakupowa) remains unchanged from prior reports: lead-generation/tender-discovery only, no bidding-to-execution-to-invoice workflow, and no wholesaler-role equivalent to Matadora's three-sided marketplace.

## 5. Suggestions

1. **Close the KSeF payment-reference gap before July 31, 2026 becomes a live compliance issue.** If Matadora's `src/lib/ksef/` module and payments flow don't yet stamp/require the KSeF invoice number on outgoing payment references, this is now a near-term (not hypothetical) compliance item — competitors with live KSeF integrations (budow.app, Comarch, InsERT) are all racing the same specific deadline.

2. **Lead with self-serve, transparent pricing as a wedge against both Polish and international competitors.** SimplyBud's implementation-fee model (from 5,000 PLN) and Procore/Buildertrend's opaque volume-quote flows both create friction at signup. BudoApka's flat 139 PLN/month remains the only fully transparent Polish comparable found across all three reports — a clear, published Matadora price point (even a simple entry tier) would differentiate against the majority of named competitors, not just BudoApka.

3. **Evaluate the Budimex accelerator (and similar GC-run programs) as a distribution channel, separate from the direct-competitor question.** Large Polish general contractors appear to be actively sourcing ConTech vendors through structured programs rather than treating it purely as a build-vs-buy internal decision — this is a potential enterprise/mid-market entry point for Matadora's contractor-side product that doesn't require winning a feature-for-feature race against budow.app or SimplyBud in the SMB segment.
