# Competitor Research: ConTech / Construction-Management SaaS in Poland & CEE

**Date:** 2026-09-14
**Author:** Automated research (Claude Code)
**Purpose:** Follow-up survey of the competitive landscape for construction-management SaaS in Poland and neighboring EU markets, benchmarked against Matadora's core modules (VAT-aware offer/estimate builder, investor↔contractor↔wholesaler marketplace, KSeF e-invoicing, multi-role dashboards). This is a follow-up to [2026-07-06](./2026-07-06.md), [2026-07-13](./2026-07-13.md), and [2026-07-27](./2026-07-27.md) — it corrects a KSeF deadline claim from the prior report, adds two Polish players not previously profiled (AI Clearing, STAS Organizer), and re-confirms international pricing.

## 1. Correction: the KSeF payment-reference deadline is split, not a single July 31, 2026 date

The 2026-07-27 report stated that the requirement to include the KSeF invoice number in payment transfers had been "postponed six months, to 31 July 2026." This cycle's research found that claim was imprecise — the actual rule is split by payment type:

- **Split-payment (mechanizm podzielonej płatności) transfers:** the KSeF number becomes a required field **from August 1, 2026**.
- **Regular (non-split-payment) transfers:** including the KSeF number stays **voluntary until December 31, 2026**, and only becomes mandatory **January 1, 2027**.

So there was no single hard deadline at July 31, 2026 that applied to all payments — that date appears to have been a misreading of the split-payment cutover (Aug 1) in the source used previously.

**Why this matters:** if Matadora's `src/lib/ksef/` module or payments flow was scoped against "mandatory for all transfers by end of July 2026," it was already ahead of the real requirement for regular transfers (which don't become mandatory until Jan 1, 2027) but needs to confirm split-payment transfers specifically are stamping the KSeF number now, since that piece of the mandate is already live.
Sources: [iFirma — numer KSeF w przelewach](https://www.ifirma.pl/blog/numer-ksef-w-elektronicznych-przelewach-bankowych-nowy-obowiazek-dla-przedsiebiorcow/), [JPKinfo — płatności a KSeF](https://jpk.info.pl/faktury/e-faktura-ustrukturyzowana-ksef/platnosci-a-ksef/), [Porównaj Księgowość — numer KSeF w przelewie 2027](https://porownajksiegowosc.pl/blog/numer-ksef-w-przelewie-2027/)

All other KSeF milestones tracked in prior reports (Feb 1, 2026 for large taxpayers >200M PLN; Apr 1, 2026 for most VAT-registered businesses; Jan 1, 2027 for micro-taxpayers under 10,000 PLN/month; offline mode available through end of 2026) are unchanged and remain in effect.

## 2. New this cycle: two more Polish players, in adjacent (not identical) niches

### AI Clearing — well-funded, but a different niche than the SMB/mid-market estimating-and-marketplace space

Prior reports (2026-07-13) noted "no Poland-specific ConTech funding was found" among VC activity. This cycle found a clear exception: **AI Clearing**, founded in Warsaw in 2020 (now HQ'd in Austin, TX, with its R&D hub still in Warsaw), raised a **$14M Series A** (Prudence, FJ Labs, Tera Ventures, Inovo, Innovation Nest) and announced a further **$16M R&D investment** in its Warsaw center in 2025. Its product uses drone imagery, an on-site mobile app, and project designs/schedules to give large infrastructure owners (highways, solar farms, airports, rail) automated daily progress-vs.-design/budget/schedule comparisons.

This doesn't compete with Matadora directly — AI Clearing targets large linear/infrastructure megaprojects and progress-verification, not SMB estimating, bidding, or invoicing — but it corrects the prior report's "Polish ConTech is unfunded" framing and shows Polish-founded ConTech can attract serious international capital when the niche (AI/automation-heavy, infrastructure-scale) is distinctive enough.
Sources: [Sifted — AI Clearing Series A](https://sifted.eu/articles/ai-clearing-series-a-14m), [AI Clearing](https://www.aiclearing.com/)

### STAS Organizer — workforce/resource management, overlaps Matadora's workforce/equipment modules, not its core estimate/marketplace loop

**STAS Organizer** is a Polish web/Android/iOS platform for construction and field-service companies covering employee management, GPS-based vehicle/tool tracking, fuel consumption, work-hour and leave tracking, task/project scheduling, and a "work diary" view across multiple active project sites. No pricing was found in this pass.

This sits closer to Matadora's `workforce.ts`, `attendance.ts`, and `equipment.ts` server-action domains than to its estimate/offer/marketplace core — it's a resource-and-crew-management tool, not an estimating or bidding platform, and shows no VAT-aware estimating, KSeF, or investor-facing features. Relevant mainly as a reminder that Matadora's workforce/equipment modules have standalone Polish competition even though its estimating+marketplace+KSeF combination does not.
Source: [STAS Organizer (Google Play)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.resser.stas)

## 3. Re-confirmed and refined findings

- **SimplyBud's pricing model is now more precisely characterized:** a **one-time implementation fee starting at 5,000 PLN plus an ongoing monthly subscription**, marketed as "no hidden costs, no long-term contracts." This refines (rather than contradicts) the 2026-07-27 report's "implementation-package-based, starting at 5,000 PLN" description — the monthly-subscription component is now confirmed to exist alongside the setup fee, not instead of it.
- **budow.app** and **BudoApka** are both confirmed still active with 2026 updates (budow.app: April 2026 content on e-dziennik budowy and KSeF; BudoApka: unchanged 139 PLN/month "6 PLN/day" flat pricing). No material feature or pricing changes found since the 2026-07-13/07-27 reports.
- **International pricing** (Procore volume-quote, Buildertrend's shift away from published tiers, PlanRadar's ~€26-129/user/month EU-priced tiers, Fieldwire's $54-104/user/month tiers, Autodesk BIM 360/ACC at $145-2,500+/user/year) is consistent with the 2026-07-27 report; no changes found this cycle. Full detail remains in that report.
- The public-tender/lead-gen marketplace landscape (Eurobudowa, Atlas Przetargów, Platforma Zakupowa, Oferent.com.pl, Biznes-Polska) and the B2B wholesale ordering platforms (3W, KIM24, Automico) are unchanged: discovery/procurement-only, no bidding-to-invoice workflow, no three-role marketplace equivalent to Matadora's.

## 4. Suggestions

1. **Verify the split-payment KSeF number requirement (live since August 1, 2026) specifically, not just the general "KSeF integration exists" claim.** Since this is the one piece of the payment-reference mandate that is already mandatory (regular transfers aren't mandatory until Jan 1, 2027), it's the most concrete near-term compliance item: confirm invoices/payments routed through split-payment mechanizm in Matadora's finance flow already carry the KSeF number, and correct any internal documentation or customer messaging that still cites the earlier (incorrect) July 31, 2026 date.

2. **Consider a workforce/equipment-module comparison against STAS Organizer**, not as a competitive threat to the core estimate/marketplace product, but as a feature-completeness check: STAS's GPS/fuel/tool-tracking and leave-management depth may exceed what Matadora's `workforce.ts`/`equipment.ts`/`attendance.ts` currently expose. If Matadora is marketing those modules as full-featured, worth confirming they hold up against a dedicated single-purpose competitor rather than only against the multi-role bundle story.

3. **The three-sided marketplace + staged VAT estimate + KSeF combination remains the clearest surviving differentiator across four research cycles now (07-06, 07-13, 07-27, and this report).** No competitor found in any pass — Polish or international, general-purpose or niche (AI Clearing, STAS Organizer included) — combines investor↔contractor↔wholesaler bidding with VAT-staged estimating and native KSeF in one workflow. With two months of consistent research turning up adjacent-but-not-overlapping players each time, this is a good signal to lean into that positioning confidently in outward-facing material rather than continuing to hedge it as provisional.
