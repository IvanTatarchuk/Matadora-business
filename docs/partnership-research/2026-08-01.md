## 1. Grupa PSB Handel S.A.

**What they do:** Poland's largest building-materials wholesale group — a cooperative
network of ~400 partner companies operating ~286 wholesale branches plus the PSB

sales (2023), roughly 15% national market share.

**Why it fits Matadora:** Matadora's `offer_materials` / procurement flow and the

PSB branches inside Matadora's marketplace) would give contractors real-time pricing
and stock data when building estimates, and give PSB a new digital demand channel from
contractors/investors already sourcing materials through Matadora's offer flow.

**Outreach angle:** Position as a low-lift pilot — a catalog/price-list feed
integration with a handful of PSB branches in one voivodeship, measured on order
