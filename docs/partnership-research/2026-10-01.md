## 1. Selena Group (Selena FM S.A.)

**What they do:** Wrocław-headquartered, Warsaw Stock Exchange-listed (since 2008)
global producer and distributor of construction chemicals — mounting foams,
sealants, adhesives, waterproofing, and insulation systems. One of the four largest
global producers of mounting foams, with 30 companies across 19 countries and
manufacturing in Poland and abroad.

insulation) are recurring line items in the Material stage of

investor-facing offers.
